## What It Does

Cjobfit brings job posting, AI resume analysis, skills assessments, voice interviews, live video interviews, and team collaboration into one platform.

**For employers** — post a job, review AI-ranked applicants, run assessments, conduct interviews, and decide as a team. Available on web.

**For candidates** — browse jobs, apply, complete assessments, record AI voice interview responses, and join live video calls. Available on web, iOS, and Android.

---

## Key Features

- **AI-Powered** — resume analysis, job description generation, interview scoring, and candidate evaluation, built with LangChain
- **Skills Assessments** — custom question sets with server-side timer and auto-scoring
- **AI Voice Interviews** — candidates record async responses; employers review transcripts and scores
- **Live Video Interviews** — scheduled video calls conducted inside the platform
- **Team Collaboration** — share applicants, collect structured evaluations, and make decisions together
- **Encrypted Messaging** — end-to-end encrypted chat between employers and candidates
- **Pay-As-You-Go** — $199 per job posting · $10 per assessment · no monthly fees

## Tech Stack
